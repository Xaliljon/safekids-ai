# Purpose

This document defines the primary and secondary users of Guardian AI products.

The purpose is to understand:

- Who uses the product
- Their goals
- Their daily workflow
- Their frustrations
- Their expectations
- How Guardian AI creates value

This document is the foundation for UX, Product Design, PRD and Feature Development.

---

# User Classification

Guardian AI users are divided into three groups.

1. Decision Makers

People who purchase the system.

2. Operators

People who use the system every day.

3. Protected Users

People who benefit from the system.

---

# Persona 1

## Kindergarten Owner

Role

Business Owner

Decision Maker

Age

30–60

Goals

- Protect children
- Increase parents' trust
- Improve reputation
- Reduce operational risks
- Expand business

Pain Points

- Hard to monitor multiple branches
- Fear of safety incidents
- Parent complaints
- Limited operational visibility

Needs

- Reliable technology
- Easy deployment
- Affordable pricing
- Reports
- Multi-branch dashboard

Success Means

Parents trust the kindergarten.

Incidents are reduced.

---

# Persona 2

## Kindergarten Director

Role

Daily Operations Manager

Primary System User

Age

28–55

Goals

- Ensure children's safety
- Respond quickly
- Supervise classrooms
- Manage staff
- Reduce operational stress

Pain Points

- Too many cameras
- Cannot watch everything
- Incidents discovered too late
- Difficult reporting

Needs

- Real-time alerts
- Simple dashboard
- Mobile notifications
- Easy review of incidents

Success Means

The system notifies only important events.

---

# Persona 3

## Teacher

Role

Child Care

Secondary User

Goals

- Keep children safe
- Focus on education
- Reduce paperwork
- Receive support during incidents

Pain Points

- Managing many children simultaneously
- Limited visibility
- Stress during busy periods

Needs

- AI should assist
- Not evaluate teachers
- Not increase workload

Important

Guardian AI supports teachers.

Guardian AI does not monitor teacher performance.

---

# Persona 4

## Parent

Role

Customer

Indirect User

Goals

- Child safety
- Peace of mind
- Trust
- Transparency

Pain Points

- Cannot see daily activities
- Fear of unnoticed incidents
- Anxiety after media reports

Needs

- Confidence
- Transparent communication
- Trustworthy kindergarten

Important

Parents do NOT receive raw surveillance access.

Privacy must be preserved.

---

# Persona 5

## System Administrator

Role

IT Management

Goals

- Install system
- Maintain cameras
- Update software
- Monitor device health

Pain Points

- Multiple hardware vendors
- Network issues
- Updates
- Storage management

Needs

- Remote management
- Health monitoring
- Diagnostics
- OTA updates

---

# Protected Users

Children

Children never directly interact with the product.

Every product decision must prioritize:

Safety

Privacy

Dignity

Respect

Children are the reason Guardian AI exists.

# Common Pain Points

Current systems:

- Passive cameras
- No intelligent alerts
- Manual monitoring
- Slow response
- Information overload

Guardian AI addresses these problems through Edge AI.

---

# User Expectations

Every user expects:

- Reliability
- Simplicity
- Privacy
- Fast alerts
- Easy learning
- Low maintenance

---

# What Users Do NOT Want

Teachers do not want surveillance.

Parents do not want privacy violations.

Directors do not want alert spam.

Owners do not want expensive infrastructure.

Administrators do not want difficult maintenance.

---

# Product Principles Based on Personas

The system should:

Notify directors.

Support teachers.

Protect children.

Respect parents.

Help owners.

Simplify administration.

---

# Persona Validation

The personas described in this document are hypotheses.

They must be validated through:

- Interviews
- Pilot deployments
- Observation
- Customer feedback

Personas will evolve over time.

---

# Success Metrics

Guardian AI succeeds when:

Directors trust alerts.

Teachers accept the system.

Owners renew subscriptions.

Parents feel confident.

Children remain safer.
