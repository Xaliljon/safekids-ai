# Guardian AI

# AI Ethics & Responsible AI Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This document defines the ethical principles governing every Artificial Intelligence system developed by Guardian AI.

These principles are mandatory.

No feature, model, experiment, or business opportunity may violate these principles.

Safety, privacy, and human dignity always take priority over automation and business objectives.

---

# Our Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence exists to assist humans.

Artificial Intelligence does not replace human judgment.

Artificial Intelligence does not replace responsibility.

Guardian AI develops Assistive AI, not Autonomous AI.

---

# Primary Mission

Our AI systems exist for one purpose:

To improve child safety through early detection of potential safety risks while preserving privacy and respecting human rights.

---

# Core Ethical Principles

## 1. Human Life Comes First

Every engineering decision must prioritize human life and physical safety.

Commercial goals never outweigh safety.

Performance never outweighs safety.

Convenience never outweighs safety.

---

## 2. Children Deserve Maximum Protection

Children are our highest priority.

Guardian AI systems shall always operate with the assumption that children's rights and privacy require stronger protection than ordinary environments.

Every AI decision must be made with this principle in mind.

---

## 3. AI Assists Humans

Guardian AI never replaces human responsibility.

AI:

- Detects
- Estimates
- Warns
- Assists

Humans:

- Review
- Verify
- Decide
- Take action

---

## 4. AI Never Accuses

Guardian AI never concludes that:

- abuse occurred
- a teacher is guilty
- a parent is guilty
- a crime happened

Instead, AI only identifies:

Potential Safety Events

Human verification is always required.

---

## 5. Human Oversight is Mandatory

Every AI-generated alert requires human review.

Guardian AI never performs:

Automatic punishment

Automatic disciplinary actions

Automatic legal decisions

Automatic employee evaluation

---

## 6. Privacy First

Privacy is fundamental.

Video should remain inside customer premises whenever technically possible.

Guardian AI should prefer:

Edge AI

Local inference

Minimal cloud processing

Encrypted storage

Minimal data retention

---

## 7. Data Minimization

Collect only the information necessary.

Do not collect data simply because it is technically possible.

Store only what is required.

Delete information when no longer needed.

---

## 8. Transparency

Users deserve to understand:

Why an alert occurred

Which AI model generated it

Confidence score

Known limitations

Guardian AI should never behave like an unexplained black box.

---

## 9. Explainability

Every significant AI decision should be explainable.

Whenever possible, the system should provide:

Observed event

Detection reason

Confidence

Timestamp

Relevant evidence

---

## 10. Fairness

Guardian AI must not intentionally discriminate based on:

Gender

Race

Ethnicity

Religion

Nationality

Language

Economic status

Any observed bias should be measured, documented, and reduced.

---

## 11. Reliability

Guardian AI should minimize:

False negatives

False positives

Unexpected behavior

Model instability

Reliability is a safety requirement.

---

## 12. Continuous Improvement

AI models are never considered finished.

Every deployment should improve:

Accuracy

Robustness

Latency

Privacy

Explainability

---

# What Guardian AI Will Never Build

Guardian AI will never intentionally develop AI systems for:

Mass surveillance

Social scoring

Emotion-based punishment

Automatic legal decisions

Secret monitoring

Unauthorized facial recognition

Unethical behavioral profiling

Products that violate children's rights

# AI Ethics Checklist

Before shipping any AI feature, ask:

✓ Does it improve safety?

✓ Does it respect privacy?

✓ Can it be explained?

✓ Can humans override it?

✓ Is human review required?

✓ Does it minimize collected data?

✓ Does it avoid unnecessary surveillance?

✓ Would we trust this system for our own children?

If any answer is NO,

the feature must not ship.
