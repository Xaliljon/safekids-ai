# Executive Summary

Guardian AI targets the rapidly growing Early Childhood Education technology market.

The initial focus is private kindergartens in Uzbekistan.

Long-term expansion includes schools, hospitals, elderly care facilities, and other safety-critical environments.

Guardian AI is positioned as an Edge AI Safety Platform rather than a surveillance company.
