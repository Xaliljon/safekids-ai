# Executive Summary

Guardian AI is a deep technology platform focused on protecting children through privacy-preserving Edge Artificial Intelligence.

The first product built on this platform is SafeKids, an AI-powered early warning system designed for kindergartens.

Unlike traditional CCTV systems, SafeKids does not simply record video.

Instead, it continuously analyzes events locally on an Edge AI device and notifies responsible personnel only when a potential safety event requires attention.

The system is designed with privacy as its primary principle.

Video should remain inside the kindergarten whenever possible.

Cloud processing is optional rather than mandatory.

Guardian AI is not a surveillance company.

Guardian AI is a child safety technology company.

# Problem Statement

Current CCTV systems have major limitations.

They:

- Record everything
- Detect nothing
- Depend entirely on human monitoring

Kindergarten directors cannot continuously observe dozens of cameras.

Parents cannot watch live feeds throughout the day.

Important incidents are often discovered only after they occur.

This delay reduces the ability to respond quickly.

---

# Our Philosophy

We do NOT build surveillance systems.

We build early warning systems.

Artificial Intelligence assists humans.

Artificial Intelligence never replaces human judgment.

Every detected event requires human verification.

---

# Core Principles

## Privacy First

Video belongs to the kindergarten.

Video should remain local whenever possible.

Cloud processing must never be mandatory.

---

## Edge AI First

AI inference runs locally.

Internet connection should not be required.

Latency should remain below one second.

---

## Human In Control

AI generates alerts.

Humans make decisions.

AI never accuses people.

AI never determines guilt.

---

## Transparency

Every AI decision must be explainable.

Users should understand why an alert occurred.

Confidence scores should always be visible.

---

## Trust Before Features

A trustworthy product is more valuable than a feature-rich product.

---

# Initial Product Scope

SafeKids Version 1 focuses only on three events.

1. Child Fall Detection

2. Child Leaves Safe Area

3. Continuous Cry Detection

Nothing else.

---

# Non Goals

Version 1 will NOT include:

- Facial recognition
- Emotion recognition
- Teacher evaluation
- Employee scoring
- Automatic abuse detection
- Cloud-only processing
