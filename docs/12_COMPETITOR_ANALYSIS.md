# Purpose

This document analyzes existing solutions in the market.

The objective is NOT to copy competitors.

The objective is to identify market gaps where Guardian AI can create unique value.

---

# Executive Summary

The market already contains many CCTV vendors and AI video analytics platforms.

However, very few solutions are specifically designed for child safety inside kindergartens.

Most existing products focus on:

- Security
- Intrusion Detection
- Vehicle Monitoring
- Retail Analytics
- Smart Cities

Guardian AI focuses on:

Early Child Safety.

## Category 2

AI Video Analytics

Examples

- BriefCam
- Avigilon
- Milestone XProtect

Strengths

- Powerful analytics
- Enterprise ready
- Scalable

Weaknesses

- Cloud oriented
- General-purpose

## Category 3

Smart Home Cameras

Examples

- Google Nest
- Arlo
- Ring

Strengths

- Good mobile experience
- Easy setup

Weaknesses

- Home use only
- No institutional workflows

## Category 4

Baby Monitoring

Examples

- Nanit
- Cubo AI

Strengths

- Child-oriented
- Sleep monitoring
- Cry detection

Weaknesses

- Designed for homes
- Single child
- No classroom support

---

## Category 5

Edge AI Platforms

Examples

- NVIDIA Metropolis
- Intel OpenVINO

Strengths

- Excellent AI infrastructure
- High performance
- Developer ecosystem

Weaknesses

- Not complete products
- Require engineering expertise

Guardian AI will use platforms like these rather than compete with them.

# What We Learn

Competitors prove that:

- Customers buy cameras.
- Customers buy AI analytics.
- Customers value automation.

However,

few companies combine:

Edge AI

+

Privacy

+

Child Safety

+

Kindergarten workflows

into a single platform.

# Conclusions

Guardian AI should not compete with surveillance companies.

Guardian AI should create a new category:

AI-Powered Child Safety Early Warning Platform.

This positioning differentiates the company while remaining technically achievable.
